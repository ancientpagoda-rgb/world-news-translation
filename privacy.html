Privacy Policy

How this site handles cookies, device information, location information, and advertising.

Last updated: November 21, 2026. This policy describes the current website and the safeguards used before advertising services are enabled.

What the site currently collects

The site does not require an account and does not intentionally collect a name, email address, precise location, or device fingerprint. It does not request access to your device location. Search and language controls run in your browser.

Cookies and local storage

The site currently uses browser local storage only to remember your privacy choice. This is a first-party preference, not an advertising profile. You can clear it through your browser settings or use .

Third-party services

The site may request resources from Google Fonts and, when speech is requested, jsDelivr-hosted speech software. Those providers may receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser, device, and requested resource. Their own policies govern those requests.

Advertising and Google AdSense

The AdSense publisher configuration is prepared, but Google AdSense is not served: the site keeps advertising disabled until account/site approval, a certified consent management platform, and verified consent controls are active. If AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may store or access cookies, device identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, and other information from your device to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Where consent is legally required, advertising will remain disabled until you choose an applicable consent option.

Google information: How Google uses information from sites and apps and Google Privacy Policy.

Before personalized advertising is enabled for users in regions where Google requires certified consent signaling, the site operator must configure a Google-certified consent management platform and verify its consent signals. The site’s local preference control is a safety gate; it is not a substitute for that regional certification.

Your choices

You may reject optional advertising storage, accept it where lawful, or change your choice later using the privacy settings control. Rejecting optional storage does not prevent access to the news site. The site will not use a rejected choice to load optional advertising technology.

Content and links

Headline links lead to independent publishers. Their privacy practices are separate from this site. For privacy questions or corrections, use the project’s GitHub repository without posting sensitive personal information.
